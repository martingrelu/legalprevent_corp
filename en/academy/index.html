Record of training completion

PDF issuance requires a completed assignment and a recorded result. It documents the training undertaken and its assessment.

What the completion record means

The training completion record is intended for information and awareness. It is not an official qualification, officially approved training, professional certification or ISO/UNE accreditation.

It must be supplemented with the organisation’s specific policies, procedures and measures. Taking a course or passing its assessment does not by itself establish compliance with the company’s training obligations.
