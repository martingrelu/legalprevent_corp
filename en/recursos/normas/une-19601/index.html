UNE 19601:2025

Criminal compliance: from identifying risks to organising responsibilities

Decisions about payments, recruitment and commercial relationships deserve attention when considering criminal risks. UNE 19601:2025 concerns criminal compliance management systems in the Spanish context.

Limits of this guide

This guide does not assess criminal liability or promise exemption from liability. Applying it to a particular case requires legal advice.

Standards are voluntary frameworks, although specific contractual or regulatory references may make them relevant. Their use requires reviewing the applicable context.

LegalPrevent is not a certification body. The indicative assessment does not evaluate ISO/UNE conformity.
