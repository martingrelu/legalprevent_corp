A management framework is not a legal guarantee

These guides do not reproduce the standards or establish conformity. LegalPrevent is not a certification body. The indicative assessment is an initial look at legal risks; it is not an ISO/UNE audit.

Each guide identifies its reference edition and links to the official organisation for the document and updates. The questions are original and help prepare a professional conversation.
