ISO/IEC 42001:2023

Questions to ask before introducing AI into a business

Introducing AI affects decisions, information and people. ISO/IEC 42001 addresses a management system for organisations providing or using products or services with AI.

Enquire with the teamIndicative assessment

A conversation to begin

Before adopting a tool, clarify its purpose and who can review its results. Consider how work would continue if a response were incorrect.

Original preparation questions

  • Which specific problem do we want to solve with AI?
  • Who decides whether a result can be used?
  • How will we detect and correct an unsuitable response?

Limits of this guide

This framework does not mean automatic compliance with the AI Act or establish that a particular system is lawful or safe.

Standards are voluntary frameworks, although specific contractual or regulatory references may make them relevant. Their use requires reviewing the applicable context.

LegalPrevent is not a certification body. The indicative assessment does not evaluate ISO/UNE conformity.
